# Nenva Call Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 — 14 November 2026**

This policy explains how Nenva Call handles information when people create accounts, send messages and make internet audio/video calls.

## 1. Information collected
Depending on the features used, Nenva Call may process:
- Account details: name, email address, phone number, password hash, verification status and policy acceptance records.
- Contacts and communications: contact relationships, message text, message timestamps, call participants, call status and call-signalling data (such as WebRTC offers, answers and network candidates).
- Technical data: IP address and request/security logs held by the hosting provider, device/app version and diagnostic information where collected by the client.
- Support information that a user chooses to send.

Passwords are stored as salted password hashes. Verification codes are stored as keyed hashes and expire. The API does not intentionally store the audio/video media stream; WebRTC media is exchanged between calling devices or through a TURN relay when needed. Network metadata may be visible to the relay provider.

## 2. How information is used
Information is used to create and secure accounts, verify email and phone ownership, find contacts, deliver and store messages, establish calls, prevent abuse, troubleshoot reliability, respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

## 3. Messages and calls
Messages are stored in the service database so they can be retrieved by conversation participants. Call-signalling records are stored to coordinate call setup. This initial service version does not claim end-to-end encryption for stored messages or calls. Do not send information that you are not comfortable transmitting through an internet communications service.

## 4. Sharing
Information may be processed by hosting/database providers, email delivery providers, SMS providers and TURN relay providers only as needed to operate, secure or support the service, or where disclosure is required by law. We do not sell personal information. Provider privacy terms and international processing locations should be reviewed before launch.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Account, contact, message and call records may remain in the database while the account is active and for a limited period afterward where needed for security, dispute resolution, backup recovery or legal compliance. The production service must publish specific retention periods and implement user-accessible account and data deletion before general release. Backups may retain deleted records until their scheduled expiry.

## 6. Security
We use password hashing, verification-code hashing, authenticated API requests and database access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Production launch requires TLS, restricted secrets, backups, monitoring, rate limits, access controls and security testing.

## 7. Your choices and rights
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to or request a copy of personal information. Contact the privacy contact published in the application to make a request. A monitored privacy email and responsible data controller details must be added before public launch.

## 8. Children
Nenva Call is not designed for children who are below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. Do not create an account on behalf of a child without the required authority and safeguards.

## 9. International users and legal compliance
Privacy rights and obligations depend on where users live and where data is processed. Before launch in Kenya or other markets, the operator must confirm its data-controller/processor roles, lawful bases, cross-border transfer safeguards, breach response and any required registrations or notices under applicable data-protection law.

## 10. Changes and contact
The current policy version and effective date should be displayed in the application. Material changes should be communicated to users. Publish a monitored privacy contact and legal operator details before public launch.

This policy is a product draft, not legal advice. Complete a data-protection impact assessment and legal review before public release.
